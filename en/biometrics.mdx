🔐 BeeSwarm uses your device's biometric authentication to protect access to your wallet. The flow may vary depending on the password manager configured on your phone.

## iOS

**Recommended setup: Passwords app (Apple's native)**

For biometrics to work correctly on BeeSwarm, we recommend using the native iOS **Passwords** app as your default manager.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="https://media.beeswarm.app/password-app.png"
    alt="iOS Passwords app"
    className="mr-auto"
    style={{ width:"29%" }}

</Frame>

To check or change the active manager:

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone
2. Go to **Passwords → Password Options**
3. Under **AutoFill from**, select **Passwords** (Apple icon)

<Warning>
  If another app is selected (such as Bitwarden, Google Password Manager, or similar), BeeSwarm's biometric authentication may be redirected to that manager. Switch to the Passwords app to ensure correct behavior.
</Warning>

After adjusting the setting, return to the app and enable biometrics normally.

## Android

On Android, the manager used is generally **Google Password Manager**, integrated into the system. On most devices no extra configuration is required.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="https://media.beeswarm.app/password-manager.png"

If biometrics is not triggered automatically, check your device's settings to see which password manager is active and make sure the autofill option is enabled.
