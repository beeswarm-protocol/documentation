# Bonus NFT

For every **12 new automated bees** added to the Hive, the Queen automatically earns a **bonus NFT**. Since the Hive has only 96 slots, a maximum of **8 bonus NFTs** can be generated per Hive.

The NFT remains inactive until it is transferred, opening two possibilities:

## Turn a worker into a linked Queen

If the NFT is transferred to a worker already registered in your Hive, she becomes a Queen while keeping the 2% reward link with you.

## Sell it to an external wallet

The NFT can be transferred to a wallet outside the Hive. This new Queen will not have a reward link with you, but the transfer costs **50 USDT** for the recipient. For reference, a Queen NFT purchased directly costs 250 USDT — the bonus NFT can be sold for an amount agreed upon by both parties.

<Warning>
For the new Queen to maintain a reward link with you, the wallet must already be registered in your Hive **before** the transfer. If it is outside the Hive, the link is not created.
</Warning>

The 12 wallets that generated the bonus are marked and cannot be counted again — each new bonus requires 12 new and automated wallets.

## Transfer restrictions

- It cannot be transferred to a Queen from another Hive
- It cannot be transferred to a worker from another Hive
- It can only be transferred to a clean (new) wallet or to a worker from your own Hive

## Becoming a Queen as a Worker

A worker can directly purchase a Queen NFT for **250 USDT**. In this case:

- The link with the original Queen remains
- **75 USDT** goes to her Queen
- **75 USDT** goes to the Hive

This makes it attractive for Queens to encourage their workers to level up — the Queen earns a 75 USDT reward for each worker who becomes a sub-Queen in her Hive.
