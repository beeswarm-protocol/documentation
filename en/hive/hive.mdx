# Hive

Each hive has **96 slots** in a large Honeycomb. Each queen can fill these spaces with worker bees, which may also become queens with their own Hive.

## Registration rules

- To register a wallet as a worker, it cannot already be part of another Hive;
- If you want to add a worker to your Hive who comes from another one, she must first be removed from the Hive she currently belongs to before joining yours;
- Queens cannot be registered in other Hives. If she was created within a Hive, she must remain there permanently. If she leaves, she will no longer belong to any Hive and will remain with a standalone Hive;
- A Queen can remove workers from her Hive at any time. Once removed, the worker's automation stops immediately and all of her assets remain under her own custody;
- A Queen cannot remove another queen from her Hive.
