## ✅ What BeeSwarm is

- ✅ **A self-custodial wallet.** Your keys, your recovery phrase and your funds stay only on your device. Only you have access to them.
- ✅ **A set of tools.** Wallet, token swaps, encrypted messaging and automated purchases, all running on smart contracts on BNB Chain.
- ✅ **Free to use.** Anyone can use it, with no invitation and no approval.
- ✅ **Transparent.** Every operation is a public transaction on the blockchain and can be verified by anyone.
- ✅ **Controlled by you.** Nothing happens without your signature. You can move, swap or withdraw your assets whenever you want.
- ✅ **Clear about fees.** Every fee in an operation is shown before you confirm: the BeeSwarm fee, the pool fee and the token's own fee, when there is one.

## ❌ What BeeSwarm is not

- ❌ **Not an investment platform.** We do not offer returns, we do not promise profits on any product and we do not manage assets.
- ❌ **Not a bank, a brokerage or a financial institution.**
- ❌ **Does not hold your funds.** We do not take deposits, we have no access to your balance and we do not hold value of any kind, not even for in-app payments.
- ❌ **Not an intermediary of value between users.** Fees and rewards are split automatically by smart contracts, according to public rules, within the same transaction you sign. What belongs to another user goes straight to their wallet, without passing through BeeSwarm.
- ❌ **Does not guarantee the value of any token.** Crypto prices go up and down, and you can lose money.
- ❌ **Cannot recover your account.** If you lose your recovery phrase, we have no way to restore your access.
- ❌ **Not an intermediary for external apps.** When you connect your wallet to a decentralized app, the connection is directly between you and that app. BeeSwarm does not take part in it, does not control it and is not responsible for what happens there.
- ❌ **Not a partner of the projects that use the wallet.**

## Projects and communities that use BeeSwarm

BeeSwarm is a digital wallet available on the internet and free to use. Because of that, any person, group or institution can adopt it, without needing permission.

So, if any project or community is using BeeSwarm, it must be clear that:

- ❌ We have no partnership, ownership stake or agreement with these projects, unless announced on our official channels.
- ❌ They do not speak on behalf of BeeSwarm.
- ❌ We are not responsible for promises of profit, investment plans or any other offer they make.

BeeSwarm is only responsible for what is published, in writing, on its official channels.

<Warning>
BeeSwarm will never ask for your recovery phrase or your private key. Be wary of anyone who does.
</Warning>
