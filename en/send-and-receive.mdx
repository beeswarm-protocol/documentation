---
title: "Send and receive"
description: "How to send and receive tokens on BeeSwarm."
---

📤 To send tokens, open the send option in your wallet, enter the recipient's address or scan their QR code, type the amount, and confirm the transaction.

🔍 Always double-check the destination address before confirming. Blockchain transactions are irreversible.

📥 To receive tokens, share your public address or show your QR code to whoever is going to send you funds. Your address can be copied or displayed directly in the app.

⛽ Every transaction requires a small network fee (gas fee), paid in BNB on the BNB Smart Chain. Make sure you have enough BNB in your balance to cover the fee.

⏱️ Transactions are processed by the blockchain network and are usually confirmed within seconds on the BNB Smart Chain.
