# Who We Are

**Tolvai Company LLC** is an American company, headquartered in Wyoming, focused on building intelligent solutions for the digital world. Our central directive is one: **simplicity**. [Learn more at tolvai.com →](https://tolvai.com)

We create and operate digital products with a focus on infrastructure, accessibility, and decentralized technology. Every product decision starts with the same question: *is this simple enough for anyone to use?*

## Our Vision

Web3 is incredible. The vast array of resources that decentralized technology offers represents one of the greatest transformations in financial and digital history. But technology that isn't accessible isn't technology for everyone — it's technology for a few.

If the tools are hard to use, people simply won't use them.

That's exactly the barrier Tolvai was built to break down: removing the friction between everyday people and the extraordinary tools Web3 has to offer.

## What We Do

We develop and operate digital products designed with a focus on **simplicity**, **autonomy**, and **modern technology**. Our work spans decentralized infrastructure, accessible interfaces, and systems that work for anyone — regardless of their level of experience with crypto.

**BeeSwarm** is our flagship product and the fullest expression of that mission.
