BeeSwarm was built to give you full control over your funds. Follow the recommendations below to keep your wallet secure.

## Non-custodial wallet

BeeSwarm is a non-custodial wallet. This means no server stores your password, seed phrase, or private keys — everything is saved locally on your device.

## Protect your seed phrase

Your seed phrase is the master key to your wallet. Write it down on paper, store it in a safe, offline place, and never share it with anyone — not even with BeeSwarm support.

<Warning>
  BeeSwarm will never ask for your seed phrase, password, or private keys via email, chat, or any other channel. Be suspicious of any message that requests this information.
</Warning>

## Enable biometric authentication

Enable biometric authentication to protect access to the app. Face ID and Touch ID ensure that only you can open your wallet.

## Use only the official address

Do not install the app from unknown links. Always access it via the official address: [mobile.beeswarm.app](https://mobile.beeswarm.app).

## Wallet recovery

If you lose or change devices, your wallet can be recovered at any time using your seed phrase.
