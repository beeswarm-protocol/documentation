BeeSwarm currently runs on the BNB Smart Chain (BSC), chosen as the main network for its speed and low fees.

## BNB Smart Chain (BSC)

BeeSwarm's main network. It supports BEP-20 tokens, including BNB, USDT, USDC, and other tokens on the network.

- **Transaction fees** — paid in BNB and usually in the order of cents.
- **WalletConnect** — connections via WalletConnect operate exclusively on the BNB Smart Chain.
