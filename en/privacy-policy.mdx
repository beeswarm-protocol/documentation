**Updated: April 2026**

BeeSwarm Wallet was designed with privacy as a fundamental principle. This document describes how we handle information related to the use of the product.

## I. General principle

BeeSwarm is a blockchain access tool. Your recovery phrase, private key, and password **are never transmitted to or stored on our servers**. All this data exists exclusively on your device, in encrypted form. BeeSwarm does not have access to it under any circumstances.

## II. What we collect

**2.1** BeeSwarm only collects aggregated and anonymous data, such as the total number of wallets created or imported. This data has no link to your identity, wallet address, or device.

**2.2** We use traffic analysis tools (such as Cloudflare Analytics) to monitor service performance and availability. These tools collect technical data in an aggregated and anonymous manner.

**2.3** We do not collect your name, email, tax ID, physical address, or any personally identifiable data.

## III. What we do not collect

BeeSwarm **does not collect, store, or have access to:**
- Your recovery phrase (seed phrase);
- Your private key;
- Your wallet password;
- Your balance or transaction history;
- Any data that allows you to be individually identified or tracked.

## IV. Public blockchain data

Transaction records are public by the nature of the blockchain and can be consulted by anyone in explorers such as BscScan or Etherscan. BeeSwarm does not control this visibility — it is inherent to the operation of blockchain networks.

## V. Third-party dApps

When you connect your wallet to third-party dApps via WalletConnect, those services operate under their own privacy policies. BeeSwarm is not responsible for data collected by third parties during these interactions.

## VI. Minors

BeeSwarm Wallet is intended exclusively for persons aged 18 or older. We do not intentionally collect data from minors.

## VII. Changes to this policy

BeeSwarm may update this Policy at any time. Changes will take effect immediately upon publication. Continued use of the product after any update will be considered acceptance of the new version.

## VIII. Contact

For questions about this Policy, please contact us by email: **contact@beeswarm.app**
