**Updated: April 2026**

BeeSwarm Wallet was designed with privacy as a fundamental principle. This document describes how we handle information related to the use of the product.

## I. General principle

BeeSwarm is a blockchain access tool. Your recovery phrase, private key, and password **are never transmitted or stored on our servers**. All this data exists exclusively on your device, in encrypted form. BeeSwarm has no access to it under any circumstances.

## II. What we collect

**2.1** BeeSwarm collects only aggregated and anonymous data, such as the total number of wallets created or imported. This data has no link to your identity, wallet address, or device.

**2.2** We use traffic analysis tools (such as Cloudflare Analytics) to monitor the performance and availability of the service. These tools collect technical data in an aggregated and anonymous manner.

**2.3** We do not collect names, emails, tax IDs, physical addresses, or any other identifiable personal data.

## III. What we do not collect

BeeSwarm **does not collect, does not store, and has no access to:**
- Your recovery phrase (seed phrase);
- Your private key;
- Your wallet password;
- Your balance or transaction history;
- Any data that allows you to be individually identified or tracked.

## IV. Public blockchain data

Transaction records are public by the nature of the blockchain and can be consulted by anyone on explorers such as BscScan or Etherscan. BeeSwarm does not control this visibility — it is inherent to how blockchain networks operate.

## V. Third-party dApps

When connecting your wallet to third-party dApps via WalletConnect, these services operate under their own privacy policies. BeeSwarm is not responsible for data collected by third parties during these interactions.

## VI. Minors

BeeSwarm Wallet is intended exclusively for persons aged 18 years or older. We do not intentionally collect data from minors.

## VII. Changes to this policy

BeeSwarm may update this Policy at any time. Changes will take effect immediately upon publication. Continued use of the product after any update will be considered acceptance of the new version.

## VIII. Contact

For questions about this Policy, contact us by email: **contact@beeswarm.app**
