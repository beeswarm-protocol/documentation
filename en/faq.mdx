<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does BeeSwarm have access to my funds?">
    No. BeeSwarm is a non-custodial wallet. Your seed phrase and private keys are stored only on your device. No server has access to them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use BeeSwarm on multiple devices?">
    Yes. Just import your wallet using your seed phrase on any device with a modern browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I lose my phone?">
    Your funds are safe. With your seed phrase, you can recover your wallet on any other device.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to create an account to use BeeSwarm?">
    No. No registration or account is required. Just create or import a wallet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does BeeSwarm charge fees?">
    BeeSwarm does not charge any fees of its own. The only fees involved are network fees (gas fee), paid directly to the blockchain.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which tokens are supported?">
    Currently all BEP-20 tokens on the BNB Smart Chain. Soon, ERC-20 tokens on Ethereum Mainnet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Via email at [contact@beeswarm.app](mailto:contact@beeswarm.app).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
