---
title: "Wallet Connect"
description: "How to connect BeeSwarm with decentralized applications using WalletConnect."
---

🔗 BeeSwarm supports WalletConnect, the standard protocol for connecting wallets to decentralized applications such as PancakeSwap, Uniswap, and other DeFi platforms.

🔍 BeeSwarm is officially listed on WalletConnect. On any dApp that supports the protocol, just search for "BeeSwarm" in the wallet list to find it.

📱 Inside the app, the WalletConnect module offers three options:

- **Scanner** — scan the QR code displayed by the dApp to connect instantly.
- **Connect via link** — copy the URI generated by the dApp and paste it directly into BeeSwarm. Useful for connecting from the same device, without needing to scan. The flow is identical to that of a wallet extension on a computer: browser open with the dApp in one tab, BeeSwarm in another.
- **Active sessions** — view all open connections and disconnect whenever you want. Disconnection can also be done from the dApp itself.

✅ After connecting, every dApp request — sign messages, confirm transactions, authorize spending — appears in BeeSwarm so you can review it before approving or rejecting.

🛡️ No action is approved automatically. You have full control over what you authorize.
