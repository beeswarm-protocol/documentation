**BeeSwarm** is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet that runs directly in your browser, with no need to install anything from an app store or extension.

It can be installed as a native app on the home screen of your phone or desktop, and it works on any device with a modern browser.

Your seed and private keys are encrypted and stored locally on your device — they are never sent to any server. You have full control over your funds.

With support for BNB Smart Chain and compatibility with WalletConnect, you can send, receive, and interact with dApps directly from BeeSwarm.
