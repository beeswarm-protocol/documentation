**BeeSwarm** is a self-custodial cryptocurrency wallet that runs directly on your device, with no need to install anything from an app store or extension. It can be added to your phone or desktop home screen and works on any device with a modern browser.

Your seed and private keys are encrypted and stored exclusively on your device — they are never transmitted to any server. You have full control over your funds.

## Built to not change under your feet

BeeSwarm was created with a clear premise: important features should always be in the same place and easy to find. No hidden menus, no buttons that move with every update. The layout is stable by design — so you learn it once and never have to relearn it.

## Integrated into the BeeSwarm ecosystem

The wallet is the center of the ecosystem. From it you access BeeChat, the Swarm, the built-in swap, and dApp connections via WalletConnect — all in a few taps, without leaving the app.

With support for BNB Smart Chain, you can send, receive, swap tokens, and interact with dApps directly through BeeSwarm.
