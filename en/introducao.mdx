🐝 BeeSwarm is an ecosystem of decentralized financial tools, built to make access to cryptocurrencies simple, secure, and accessible for everyone.

The ecosystem is composed of two main modules:

**👝 Wallet** A non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet that runs directly in your browser, with no need to install anything from an app store. Your funds, your seed and your private keys remain exclusively on your device.

**🍯 Swarm** A distribution and cashback mechanism that lets users actively participate in the ecosystem's growth. Documentation coming soon.
