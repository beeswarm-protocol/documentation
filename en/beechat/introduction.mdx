BeeChat is the native private communication layer of the BeeSwarm ecosystem. Designed to provide digital sovereignty, it enables P2P (Peer-to-Peer) message exchange between digital wallets, eliminating intermediaries and centralized servers. BeeChat's architecture ensures that privacy is not a feature, but the fundamental premise of connection.

With end-to-end encryption, the message is encrypted at the moment it is sent by the sender and only decrypted in the recipient's wallet. Message security is at a military-grade level.

## Why BeeChat Is Different

Most messaging apps store your conversations on company servers. Even if they promise encryption, the data passes through — and stays on — infrastructure that is not yours.

### Self-Sovereign Identity

In BeeChat, your wallet is your identity. There is no registration, email forms, or centralized passwords. Your public address is your unique identifier. Message viewing is restricted to the device where the wallet is active. By design, the system operates under the premise of access ephemerality: if the wallet is removed from the device, the decryption key is discarded, making the content permanently inaccessible. Privacy is guaranteed by the technical impossibility of recovering data without the original private key.

BeeChat in a nutshell:

- **No message server** — no company, including BeeSwarm, stores or accesses the content of your conversations
- **End-to-end encryption** — the message is encrypted on your device before it leaves. Only the recipient can decrypt it
- **Blockchain as backend** — messages live on BNB Smart Chain, permanently and with no possibility of removal
- **Your wallet is your identity** — no registration, username, or password. You are your address

<Note>
  BeeChat is only accessible within BeeSwarm. You will need an active BeeWallet wallet to use it.
</Note>

## BeeChat's Advantages Over Other Apps

Unlike apps like **Signal** — a privacy reference — BeeChat does not rely on any server to deliver messages. Content goes directly to the blockchain, with no intermediary, no centralized infrastructure that can be taken down, censored, or compromised.

Unlike **WhatsApp** and other popular messaging apps, BeeChat does not require an account, phone number, email, or any personal data. Your identity is your wallet — and your data does not exist on any third-party server.
